Switch to the mini player with **Ctrl+M** (**Cmd+Shift+M** on macOS), the
shrink button in the main window, or **Settings > Mini player > Switch to it**.

![The modern mini player](/assets/mini-player-review/after-dark.png)

The player uses the main window's colors and fonts. It shows album art, the
song and artist, saved status, playback controls, and a seek bar. The full
layout also offers shuffle, repeat, volume, the active device, and a queue
button that returns to the main window's queue. Remote devices that do not
support volume control disable the volume controls.

Use the chevron to switch **Compact view** on or off, or double-click the
header. The compact layout keeps artwork, track information, seeking, and
previous/play/next controls. Drag the header to move the window.

The pin button toggles **Always on top** on supported desktops. The expand
button returns to the main window. Closing follows your existing background
playback preference. Keyboard playback shortcuts work in both layouts.

**Settings > Mini player** contains Compact view, Always on top, and Show in
taskbar. Taskbar visibility is supported on Windows and Linux X11. Wayland
cannot control the always-on-top state. Old mini player position and compact
preferences are preserved. Winamp skins and MilkDrop are no longer offered.
